Mathematical underpinnings

This viewer is built utilizing the Angular Overlap Method (AOM).

Here we assume that the total orbital energy can be calculated as the sum of the individual interactions between each ligand and the orbital. Expressible as:

1 2

Where the orbital interactions (term in above equation here) are the overlap of the ligand p and metal 3d orbitals. Here we further assume that each ligand is the same distance away from the metal center. This simplifies the orbital overlap to be based soley on ligands angular distance from the 3d orbitals.

For the orbital rendering the 3d hydrogenic wavefunctions are sampled in 3d space and used to generate an isosurface. (Should verify if there are any important prefactor differences I'm missing between the orbitals)
